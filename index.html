Adding a widget to your Home Screen

  1. Make a widget in Framed and pick your photos.
  2. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  3. Tap Edit → Add Widget (on iOS 17, tap + in the top corner).
  4. Search for Framed and choose a size.
  5. To pick which of your widgets it shows, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

Common questions

My widget didn’t change photo on time. iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so a change can arrive a few minutes late, especially in Low Power Mode.

Some photos couldn’t be added. Photos stored only in iCloud need an internet connection to download. Check your connection and add them again.

Album and On This Day widgets aren’t updating. They need access to your photo library: Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Framed. Opening Framed also refreshes them.

Do my photos get uploaded? No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
